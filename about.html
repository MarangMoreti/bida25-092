Our Story

Baking happiness, one cake at a time since 2018

How It All Began

Mtee's Cakehouse started with a simple dream and a small kitchen. What began as baking for family and friends quickly grew into something much bigger. The love for creating beautiful, delicious cakes turned into a passion that couldn't be contained.

In 2018, we opened our first small storefront in Gaborone with just one display case and big dreams. Word spread fast. Soon, we went from baking a few cakes a week to dozens of custom orders for birthdays, weddings, and every celebration in between.

Today, Mtee's Cakehouse is known for quality, creativity, and consistency. Every cake that leaves our kitchen is made with the same love and attention to detail as that very first cake baked years ago.

Meet Our Master Baker

Martha "Mtee" Thomo

With over 12 years of baking experience and training from some of the finest pastry schools, Mtee brings passion and expertise to every creation. Her journey began in her grandmother's kitchen, where she learned that baking is about more than just recipes - it's about bringing joy to people's special moments.

Mtee specializes in custom celebration cakes, wedding cakes, and unique dessert designs. Her work has been featured in local magazines and at major events across Botswana.

"Every cake tells a story. I'm honored to be part of your celebrations."

- Mtee
